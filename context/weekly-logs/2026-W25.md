## Stalls and Blockers

One blocker dominated the week, and it was structural rather than incidental. For five consecutive mornings the dirty working tree did not merely make reconciliation a no-op, it actively prevented daily-flow from committing anything at all. The mechanism was a deadlock: a human board reorganization set `c3844.priority` to null, the `require_kanban` pre-commit guard treats that as a hard error ("c3844 must stay priority 1 or critical"), and because the guard fires on every commit the pipeline could not even land the one-line fix that would have cleared it. That is the defining shape of the stall, a guard meant to protect an invariant becoming a deadlock once the invariant was violated by uncommitted work the guard itself blocked from being corrected. Two secondary irritants recurred underneath it. Roughly 5,000 regenerated files under `.claude/smoke-verify-install` and `.claude/smoke-verify-runs` churned the tree on every run and were repeatedly flagged for gitignoring. And 64 stale `claude/*` branch refs sat unmerged under an explicit do-not-touch-blind note; as of this Monday none are actually ahead of `dev`, so the live risk is ref clutter, not lost work. The week's takeaway is concrete: a pre-commit guard that an in-flight edit can trip needs an escape valve, or its only exit is manual and the automation stalls until a human intervenes.

## Path Not Taken

Several shortcuts were available all week, and each was deliberately declined. daily-flow never used `--no-verify` to force its logs past the guard, never edited `tools/kanban/state.json` to clear the `c3844` priority on Mike's behalf, and never touched the protected 1,226/474 board reorganization sitting in the tree; the standing rule held that the human's in-flight work is left exactly as found, even at the cost of a multi-day stall. The guard-relaxation option, loosening `require_kanban` so that done cards need no priority, was recorded on 06-16 as a genuine alternative but not taken, because the cleaner fix was restoring the one-line priority and committing in coherent groups rather than weakening an invariant to route around a symptom. And once the tree was finally clean, the 64 stale `claude/*` branches were still left unconsolidated, honoring the do-not-merge-blind note rather than batch-merging refs whose provenance had not been reviewed.
